Recon v2 · theme directions

Same app. Three languages.

One layout and one content set, three genuinely different treatments of type, colour, density and texture. Pick the language, not the arrangement. Static boards; nothing animates.

A

Nocturne

Editorial dark. Serif display, airy rhythm, violet action, quiet hairlines.

 B

Graphite

Instrument dark. Condensed grotesque, acid action, dense rules and technical grid.

 C

Ledger

Warm paper. Newsreader display, vermilion action, print rules and generous margins.

Each board shows the same header, live strip, match row, stat table, field, empty state, buttons and one card that earns its border. Design rules: docs/v2/design-system.md. IA: docs/v2/ia.md.
